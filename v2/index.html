The loop LIVE

Cost & schedule, reconciling in real time

A site progress update doesn't wait for month end to matter. It moves through the schedule, forces a new cost forecast, runs a Monte Carlo pass against both, and lands in the report someone actually reads — with a person validating each stage it passes through.

The loop LIVE

Risk, triggered from either direction

A schedule slip and an outside event — a supplier delay, a weather pattern, a code change — both land in the same system of record. The risk register doesn't wait to be asked; it reconciles, re-simulates, and reports, with a named person validating each stage.

Why this is governed, not just fast

The confirmation is the decision.

In most agentic tools, governance is the tax paid for automation — bolted on after the agent already acts. For Lognos the confirmation step and the decision moment are the same event. The loop runs continuously so that when you're asked, you're asked at the moment that matters, with a current picture.
